Delete your account

Wholesale Khata · Effective 23 November 2026

You can delete your Wholesale Khata account here, or in the app: Settings → Account & plan → Delete account.

What is deleted: your login, your account record (name, shop name, mobile, email, trial and plan dates) and your online shop with its price lists, customer requests and orders — straight away.
What is kept: Google Play / App Store purchase records (order number, product, date) for up to 8 years, as tax law requires.
Not affected: the bills and khata on your phone (erase them in the app or by uninstalling it), and a Google Play or App Store subscription — cancel that in the store.

Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password?" on the app's sign-in screen, or email rahulg.aice@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete it within 7 days.

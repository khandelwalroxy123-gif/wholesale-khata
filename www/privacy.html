Privacy policy

Wholesale Khata · Effective 23 November 2026

Wholesale Khata is a billing, stock and khata app for wholesale shops, provided by Rahul Gupta, Jaipur, Rajasthan. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android or in a web browser. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Google Play's rules.

The short version

  • You sign in with an email and password. Your account details (name, shop name, mobile, email) and your trial and plan dates are kept online so the app knows your plan on any phone.
  • Your books (bills, stock, khata, staff) stay on your own phone or computer. We cannot see them.
  • Only if a shop turns on Online orders, the shop's price list and its customers' order details are kept online so customers can order and track.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

Your account

  • What we keep: your name, shop name, mobile number, email address, an encrypted password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it), the date the account was made, when the app was last opened, app version and whether you use Android or the web.
  • Plans and payments: your trial and plan end dates, and for store purchases the product, order or transaction number, purchase date and the store's purchase token. Payment itself is handled by Google Play or Apple's App Store; we never receive your card, bank or UPI details.
  • Why: to sign you in, run the free trial and plans, give support and keep records that tax law requires.
  • Who sees it: you, and the app's administrator (us) for support and plan management. The administrator can see account and plan details and, if you use Online orders, your online shop, price list and online orders. The administrator can never see your bills, khata or stock, which stay on your device.

Information the app stores on your device

The app stores the information you enter: your shop details, products, rates, stock, bills, parties and vendors (names, phone numbers, GSTIN, balances), payments, cheques, expenses, and staff attendance and salary. It is saved only in the app's private storage on your device so that it works without internet. We cannot see or access it.

Online orders (optional)

A shop can turn on Online orders and share a link or QR code. When this is on:

  • Shop: the shop name, phone number, address, UPI ID and the retail and wholesale price lists are stored online so customers can see them.
  • Customers: when you open a shop's link, you give your name and mobile number to ask the shop for permission to order. When you order, your address (for delivery), note, items, order amount, payment method, payment status and the UPI reference number you enter are stored with the order. This is used only so the shop can accept, bill, deliver and confirm payment, and so you can track the order. Customers do not need an account; each phone or browser gets a random anonymous ID so it can see only its own orders.
  • When the shop accepts an order, the bill is saved in the shop's books on the shop's phone.
  • UPI payments are made in your own UPI app directly to the shop; the app never sees your bank details or UPI PIN.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows an alert on your phone when a customer asks to order or places an order. These alerts are made on your phone from your own online orders; no advertising or marketing notifications are sent.

Where data is stored

Account and online-order data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, servers in India where available) and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google acts as our service provider (data processor). The app keeps a copy on your device so it opens quickly and works offline. Data is not shared with anyone else and not used for advertising.

Microphone and voice entry

The microphone is used only when you tap Speak in the voice assistant. Your phone's own speech recognition service turns the words into text (on most Android phones this is provided by Google). The app receives only the text and does not keep or send any audio.

Fingerprint

If you turn on fingerprint unlock, your fingerprint is checked by Android. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint; it only learns whether the check passed.

Things you choose to share

  • Share on WhatsApp and payment reminders open WhatsApp with the message filled in. You decide whether to send it.
  • Save backup file creates a file with all your app data and opens your phone's share menu, so you can save it where you choose. That copy is then handled by the app you chose.
  • Print / PDF opens Android's print screen. Copying the GST register for your CA puts the table on your phone's clipboard.

How long we keep data, and deleting it

  • Delete your account any time: in the app (Settings → Account & plan → Delete account) or on the delete account page. This deletes your login, your account record and your online shop with its orders straight away. Google Play / App Store purchase records (order number, product, date) are kept for up to 8 years because tax and accounting law requires it, and then deleted.
  • Books on your device stay until you remove them: Settings → Start fresh, the "also erase" option when deleting your account, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not touched.
  • A customer can ask the shop, or write to us, to delete their orders and details; we will delete them within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent (by deleting your account), and name someone to act for you. Write to the grievance officer below. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

The app is made for shop owners aged 18 or over and is not directed at children.

Security

Use a strong password and a PIN others cannot guess. Data online is protected by access rules so each shop sees only its own account and orders. Keep backup files safe, because anyone with a backup file can read its contents.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date, and important changes will be shown in the app.

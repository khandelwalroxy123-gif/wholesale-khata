Privacy policy

Wholesale Khata · Effective 23 November 2026 · updated for online orders

Wholesale Khata is a billing, stock and khata app for wholesale shops. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android or in a web browser.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • Your books (bills, stock, khata, staff) stay on your own phone or computer.
  • Only if a shop turns on Online orders, the shop's price list and its customers' order details are kept in the app's online database (Google Firebase) so customers can order and track.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

Information the app stores

The app stores the information you enter: your shop details, products, rates, stock, bills, parties and vendors (names, phone numbers, GSTIN, balances), payments, cheques, expenses, and staff attendance and salary. It is saved only in the app's private storage on your device so that it works without internet. We, the developer, cannot see or access it.

Online orders (optional)

A shop can turn on Online orders and share a link or QR code. When this is on:

  • Shop: the shop name, phone number, address, UPI ID and the retail and wholesale price lists are stored online so customers can see them.
  • Customers: when you open a shop's link, you give your name and mobile number to ask the shop for permission to order. When you order, your address (for delivery), note, items, order amount, payment method, payment status and the UPI reference number you enter are stored with the order. This is used only so the shop can accept, bill, deliver and confirm payment, and so you can track the order.
  • Each phone or browser gets a random anonymous ID from Google Firebase so it can see only its own orders. No e-mail or password is used.
  • The data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, servers in India where available) and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google acts as our service provider. It is not shared with anyone else and not used for advertising.
  • When the shop accepts an order, the bill is saved in the shop's books on the shop's phone.
  • UPI payments are made in your own UPI app directly to the shop; the app never sees your bank details or UPI PIN.

Deleting online data: a shop can delete all of its online data at any time (Online orders → Delete online shop). A customer can ask the shop, or write to the developer at the e-mail address on the app's Google Play page, to delete their orders and details; we will delete them within 30 days.

Microphone and voice entry

The microphone is used only when you tap Speak in the voice assistant. The words you say are turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google and covered by Google's privacy policy). The app receives only the text, uses it to fill in the entry on your phone, and does not keep or send any audio.

Fingerprint

If you turn on fingerprint unlock, your fingerprint is checked by Android. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint; it only learns whether the check passed.

Things you choose to share

Some buttons hand information to another app only when you tap them:

  • Share on WhatsApp and payment reminders open WhatsApp with the message filled in. You decide whether to send it.
  • Save backup file creates a file with all your app data and opens your phone's share menu, so you can save it to Google Drive, WhatsApp, e-mail or your computer. That copy is then handled by the app you chose.
  • Print / PDF opens Android's print screen.
  • Copying the GST register for your CA puts the table on your phone's clipboard.

Internet use

The app works without internet. It connects to the internet only for Online orders, if the shop turns them on. Fonts are included in the app. Links to WhatsApp open in WhatsApp or your browser only when you tap them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. More → Settings → Start fresh removes your bills, payments, expenses, parties, vendors and staff from the app; for safety the app keeps a few recent copies in its private storage on the phone. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in Android settings) deletes all of its data, including those copies. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not touched; delete them where you saved them.

Children

The app is made for shop owners and is not directed at children.

Security

The app opens only with your PIN (and fingerprint, if you turn it on). Please choose a PIN that others cannot guess, and keep your backup files in a safe place, because anyone with a backup file can read its contents.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about privacy, write to the developer at the e-mail address shown on the app's Google Play page.

Refund & cancellation

Wholesale Khata · Effective 23 November 2026

This policy explains cancellations and refunds for Wholesale Khata plans. It is part of the Terms & Conditions.

Free trial

The first 30 days are free and need no payment, so try everything before you pay. Nothing is charged when the trial ends; the app simply becomes view only until you choose a plan.

Monthly plan (₹499/month)

  • Cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Wholesale Khata → Cancel (Android), in Settings → your name → Subscriptions (iPhone), or from the app (Settings → Account & plan → Manage in Google Play).
  • After you cancel, no further months are charged. The plan stays active until the end of the month already paid for. Part-months are not refunded.
  • Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel the subscription by itself.

5-Year Plan (₹20,000, one time)

  • Valid for five years from the purchase date. It is not a lifetime plan and does not renew.
  • If you ask within 7 days of purchase, we will refund it in full.
  • After 7 days the plan is generally non-refundable. If the app has a serious problem that we cannot fix, write to us and we will consider a fair partial refund for the unused period.

How refunds are paid

  • Purchases made through Google Play are refunded through Google Play to the original payment method. You can also ask Google directly under Google Play's refund policy; usually Google handles requests made within 48 hours of purchase.
  • Purchases made on iPhone are refunded by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund App Store purchases ourselves, but we will support your request.
  • Purchases made on iPhone are refunded by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund App Store purchases ourselves, but we will support your request.
  • Payments made to us in another agreed way are refunded to the same bank account or UPI ID within 7 working days of approval.
  • When a plan is refunded, it ends and the account returns to view only.

Double or wrong charges

If you were charged twice or charged after cancelling on time, write to us with the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) or the App Store order ID from your Apple receipt and we will put it right.
